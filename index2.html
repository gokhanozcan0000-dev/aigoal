frequently asked questions
What time does NASDAQ open?
NASDAQ opens at 9:30 AM Eastern Time (ET) Monday through Friday, excluding market holidays. Pre-market trading begins at 4:00 AM ET, and after-hours trading runs until 8:00 PM ET.
Is the stock market open on weekends?
No. Major stock exchanges including NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE and most global markets are closed on Saturdays and Sundays. Some cryptocurrency exchanges and OTC markets operate 24/7.
Why does the Shanghai Stock Exchange have a lunch break?
The SSE (Shanghai Stock Exchange) observes a midday break from 11:30 AM to 1:00 PM CST. Trading sessions are 9:30–11:30 AM and 1:00–3:00 PM local time. The Tokyo Stock Exchange (TSE) also has a lunch break from 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM JST.
What is the difference between pre-market and after-hours trading?
Pre-market trading occurs before the regular session (4:00–9:30 AM ET). After-hours trading happens after the close (4:00–8:00 PM ET). Both have lower liquidity and wider spreads than regular hours. Not all brokers offer access to extended hours trading.
What time is the London Stock Exchange open?
The London Stock Exchange (LSE) is open from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM GMT/BST, Monday through Friday. During British Summer Time (BST, late March–late October) this corresponds to 3:00 AM–11:30 AM ET.
